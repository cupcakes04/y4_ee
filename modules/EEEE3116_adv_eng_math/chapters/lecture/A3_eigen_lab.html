1 · Sweep the input, watch the output

Turn the input arrow x round the circle. For most directions Ax points somewhere else, so the matrix both turns and stretches. At two directions the arrows line up: A is just an amplifier there. Those are the eigenvectors, and the stretch is k. (A3 sl.5–7, 13)

2 · Solve Ax = b in eigen axes

Solving Ax = b directly is the hard direction. In eigen axes A is diagonal, so solving it is just division. (A3 sl.24–26; works because X is orthogonal for symmetric A, sl.26)

3 · Zoom into move 1: why d = Xᵀb

Question: how much of b is along axis 0? Answer: drop a line from the tip of b straight onto the axis, meeting it at a right angle, like a shadow. Where it lands is d₀. Do the same for axis 1 to get d₁.

For a length-1 axis, that shadow length is exactly the dot product: d₀ = e₀·b. Xᵀ has the axes as its rows, so Xᵀb is both dot products done in one multiply. Drag the tip of b and watch the numbers.
