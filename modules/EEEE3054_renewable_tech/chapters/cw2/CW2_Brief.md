## Scenario

Many rural and remote communities around the world do not have access to an electrical grid but still require a reliable electricity supply for essential services such as healthcare.

In this case study, you are required to develop an outline design for a renewable-energy-based electrical system to supply an off-grid health centre. The health centre must be supplied entirely by an off-grid electricity system. The electricity demand of the health centre is provided in **Figure 1**. You may assume that the same daily load profile occurs every day throughout the year.

Continuity and reliability of supply are critical. Your system design must therefore consider intermittency of renewable energy sources and the role of energy storage in maintaining security of supply.

## Task

### 1. Energy Supplies

Investigate the climate and renewable-energy resources available at your assigned location and provide a high-level assessment of their potential. Your assessment should consider **all renewable energy sources covered in the module**, including sources that may have limited or negligible potential at your chosen location.

You should provide evidence to demonstrate why a particular resource is or is not suitable. For example, if hydropower is considered unsuitable because of limited rainfall or water availability, provide relevant data to demonstrate this rather than simply excluding hydropower from the assessment.

Use figures, graphs, maps and tables where appropriate to present the available resource data. Examples include:
- annual solar irradiance;
- monthly precipitation or rainfall;
- wind-resource data;
- geothermal-resource maps; and
- other relevant resource indicators.

Based on your assessment, select the most appropriate renewable energy source, or combination of sources, for supplying the health centre. Clearly identify the preferred source(s) and justify your decision using both qualitative and quantitative evidence. You must also select a **second renewable energy source** and use it as a basis for comparison with your preferred option.

Resource data should be obtained from reliable meteorological, climate and renewable-energy databases, websites and atlases. All sources must be appropriately referenced.

### 2. Energy Conversion

Develop an outline design of the energy conversion and storage system required to supply the health centre. Your design should specify the major components of the system, which may include:
- renewable-energy generators;
- power-electronic converters and/or inverters;
- energy-storage systems;
- generators or other backup supplies, where required; and
- other relevant system components.

The proposed system should be presented using a **system diagram** developed specifically for your design. The diagram should clearly show the energy flow and the main components of the proposed system. Include the relevant conversion efficiencies and calculate the approximate electrical power and/or energy available at the appropriate stages of the system.

The renewable-energy generation and energy-conversion equipment must be appropriately sized to meet the electrical requirements of the health centre. The required energy-storage capacity must also be calculated and justified based on the energy demand and availability of the renewable resource.

Provide the basic technical specifications of the major equipment used in your proposed system.
