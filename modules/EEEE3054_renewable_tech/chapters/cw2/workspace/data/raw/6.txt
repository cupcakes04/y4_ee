point 1:

Baseline Diesel Generation Economics

Stand-alone diesel generation in remote and off-grid settings is typically among the most expensive forms of electricity supply. Studies cited by the World Bank and ESMAP indicate that the levelized cost of diesel-generated electricity commonly falls within the range of approximately US$0.35-0.70/kWh, depending on fuel prices, transport costs, load factors, and maintenance requirements.
