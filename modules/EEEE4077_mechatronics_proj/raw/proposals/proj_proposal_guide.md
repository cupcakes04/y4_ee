# Report information
The purpose of this assessment is to demonstrate clear planning for your project. The project is an enormous undertaking, and you will invest significant time in this work. To be successful, this effort needs to be well-directed. A good plan will allow you to make the most of your project.
You need to develop a clear specification for what needs to be achieved in your project and consider the methods you will need to meet the specification successfully.

The softcopy of the project proposal should be submitted through Moodle. This document should be between 7-8 pages (excluding Cover page, Table of Contents, References and Appendices). The body of the report must use the A4 page format; the text should be single-spaced, font Times Roman 12 pt (or other San Serif font of equivalent size) with a margin of 2.54 cm all around each page. The report should contain the following:

1. **Cover page**: Contains the title of your project, your name and student ID, and the supervisor's name (use the template provided under the Resources Section on this Moodle page).
2. **Overview of the Project**: Includes a clear description of the purpose of the project and provides a block diagram that encompasses the main elements of the project and shows how they relate to each other, supported by literature where possible.
3. **Specification**: Concise and complete account of the aims and objectives of the project and, where applicable, supported by literature. This can include additional “stretch” requirements that depend on things going well/being ahead of schedule.   Also, to provide the assessment method and the expected deliverables for each specification point.
4. **Methodology**: Includes discussion of what methods or processes are needed to complete the project, including tools/software required, supported by literature where possible.
5. **Originality**: Demonstrates through a short discussion how the project demonstrates originality on the part of the student and positions it within the current research/industrial context.
6. **Risk Management**: Includes a risk register (summary table) to cover the risks associated with the project and how these can be mitigated. Note that these are not Health & Safety risks but risks to the project itself (such as ordering delays, failures in equipment, implementation, etc.).
7. **Planned work schedule (Gantt Chart)**: Includes a comprehensive time plan, displaying the tasks, milestones, reflections points of risk and defined points for progress reviews (these are to be agreed upon with your supervisor – 5 in total throughout your project).
8. **A list of References**; and
9. **Appendix**: Contains the project specification given to you by the supervisor.

> Please upload your submission to the Submission Box available on this Moodle page. You must hit “Submit” on your final submission for your work to be accepted!

 

# Defence information

You will meet your Moderator for a Defence interview where you will present your project and plan and answer questions from your Moderator.  It is YOUR responsibility to arrange this interview two weeks in advance (see Module Timetable).

The presentation you prepare should contain **4 slides only**:

- One slide with the title of the project and block diagram.
- One slide describing methods and approach.
- One slide showing the Risk Register/Table.
- One slide showing the Planned Work Schedule (Gantt Chart).

These slides can be created directly from the content in your Project Proposal.
