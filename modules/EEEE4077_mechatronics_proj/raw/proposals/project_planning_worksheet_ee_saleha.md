# Project Planning & Execution Proposal Worksheet

**Author:** Ms Saleha Abdul Rahman  
**Source Document:** `Project Planning worksheet for EE - Saleha.docx`  
**Purpose:** Guiding worksheet for structuring the undergraduate Electrical & Electronic / Mechatronics Engineering Final Year Project Proposal.

---

## 1. Project Overview
- **What is the purpose of your project?**
- **Sketch or describe the block diagram of your project** (main components and their relationships).

---

## 2. Specification
- **List the main aims and objectives of your project.**
- **What are your stretch goals** if things go ahead of schedule?
- **What deliverables will you produce** for each objective?

---

## 3. Methodology
- **What methods or processes will you use** to complete the project?
- **List the tools or software required.**
- **Provide any literature or references** supporting your methodology.

---

## 4. Originality
- **How does your project demonstrate originality?**
- **How does it relate to current research or industry trends?**

---

## 5. Risk Management
- **Identify at least three risks** to your project and how you plan to mitigate them.
