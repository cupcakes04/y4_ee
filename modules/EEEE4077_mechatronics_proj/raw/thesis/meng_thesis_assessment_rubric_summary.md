## 2. Detailed Rubric Criteria & Competency Areas

### A. Specification and Validation
* **Target Specification:** Clear, measurable engineering specification establishing target goals and operational criteria.
* **Validation Methodology:** Robust validation strategies to verify whether specifications and deliverables have been achieved.
* **Engineering Impact:** Consideration of real-world constraints, user/industry requirements, and application impact.

### B. System Design and Implementation [~40%]
* **Engineering Decisions:** Sound justification for chosen methods, tools, algorithms, or hardware architectures.
* **Implementation Quality:** Rigorous execution meeting the defined technical specification.
* **Problem Solving & Innovation:** Effective identification and resolution of engineering challenges encountered during development.

### C. Presentation of Findings, Analysis & Literature [~30%]
* **Results & Discussion:** Thorough, objective presentation of experimental or simulation results.
* **Critical Evaluation:** Meaningful interpretation of findings, identification of limitations, and comparison with target benchmarks.
* **Contextualization:** Integration with existing published literature and state-of-the-art benchmarks.

### D. Reflection on Management and Professional Journey [~10%–25%]
* **Project Management:** Evaluation of timelines, milestones, risk mitigation, and contingency planning.
* **Professional Engineering Journey:** Personal reflection on learning, professional ethics, sustainability, and standards.

### E. Communication Quality and Documentation
* **Document Structure & Flow:** Adherence to thesis formatting guidelines (A4, 12 pt, margins, captioning).
* **Technical Communication:** Professional, concise, justified technical writing and clear diagrammatic presentation.
* **Referencing:** Consistent IEEE citation and reference list standards.

### F. Portfolio and Self-Management (Supervisor & Moderator Assessment)
* **Independence & Initiative:** Level of student autonomy in problem formulation and execution.
* **Professional Conduct:** Consistent engagement, progress tracking, and professional self-direction.

## 3. Achievement Criteria Scale (IET AHEP4 Mappings)

Each section is evaluated against module learning outcomes and accredited engineering competencies:

| Level | Performance Standard | Criteria Descriptor |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Not Met / Unsatisfactory** | Fails to satisfy fundamental requirements; major omissions in evidence or method. |
| **2** | **Partly Met / Developing** | Basic requirements partially addressed; inconsistent execution or shallow justification. |
| **3** | **Mostly Met / Good** | Solid, competent work satisfying standard expectations with minor gaps or oversights. |
| **4** | **Fully Met / Outstanding** | Thorough, rigorous, and professional execution exceeding baseline requirements; exemplary engineering judgment. |
| **5** | **Exceeded / Exceptional** | Publisable standard or innovative engineering achievement demonstrating master’s-level mastery. |
