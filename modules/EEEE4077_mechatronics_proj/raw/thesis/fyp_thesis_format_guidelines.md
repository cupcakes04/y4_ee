# EEEE4055 / EEEE4077 Final Year Project Thesis Guidelines

**Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering**

**University of Nottingham (UK | CHINA | MALAYSIA)**

*Academic Session: 2026–2027*

## 1. Overview and Objectives

This assessment aims to demonstrate your achievements in your project in a professional document, describing how you delivered on your target specification and the engineering choices made in doing so.

To be successful:

* Structure your effort well, with explicit reference to the marking rubric.

* Allow the reader to accurately judge the progress and decisions made during the course of your project.

* Present a clear specification for what needs to be achieved.

* Underpin chosen methods to meet the specification successfully.

* Detail how you validated this success.

* Include reflections on project management and your learning journey as a professional engineer.

## 2. Thesis Length

* **Maximum Limit:** **60 pages** for the main body.

* **Inclusions:** Inclusive of references.

* **Exclusions:** Excludes front matter and appendices.

* **Supporting Data:** Secondary or supporting data must be placed in the appendices to keep the main text concise and focused.

## 3. Required Document Structure

### Front Matter

1. **Title Page** — Must use the official template provided in the Resources section on Moodle.

2. **Abstract** — Maximum 1 page long.

3. **Acknowledgements**

4. **Table of Contents**

5. **List of Figures**

6. **List of Tables**

7. **List of Abbreviations** *(if applicable)*

### Suggested Chapter Structure

1. **Chapter 1: Introduction**

2. **Chapter 2: Literature Review**

3. **Chapter 3: Methodologies / System Design and Implementation**

4. **Chapter 4: Results and Discussion**

5. **Chapter 5: Conclusion and Future Work**

6. **References**

7. **Appendices**

## 4. Formatting Guidelines

| **Parameter** | **Specification** | 
| **Paper Size** | A4 | 
| **Font** | Times New Roman, 12 pt *(smaller fonts permitted for tables and diagrams)* | 
| **Line Spacing** | Single | 
| **Margins** | 1 inch (2.54 cm) on all sides | 
| **Text Alignment** | Justified body text | 
| **Headings** | No rigid numbering style mandated, but numbering across topics and sub-topics must remain strictly consistent | 

### Page Numbering

* **Front Matter:** Lower-case Roman numerals (`i`, `ii`, `iii`, ...)

* **Main Contents:** Arabic numerals (`1`, `2`, `3`, ...)

### Figures and Tables

* **Alignment:** Centered.

* **Numbering Scheme:** Numbered systematically (e.g., *Figure 3.1*, *Table 2.1*).

* **Caption Placement:**

  * **Figures:** Caption placed **below** the figure.

  * **Tables:** Caption placed **above** the table.

* **Caption Formatting:**

  * **Single-line captions:** Centered.

  * **Multi-line captions:** Justified.

### Referencing

* **Style:** **IEEE referencing style** is recommended.

* **Consistency:** Ensure citations and reference lists remain strictly consistent throughout the document.
