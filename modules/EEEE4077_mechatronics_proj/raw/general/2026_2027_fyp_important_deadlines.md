| **1.5** | Midterm Feedback Meeting (0%, but is a compulsory requirement) | Deadline | Compulsory progress meeting. Presentation slides to be uploaded to Moodle after the meeting. | Either end of Autumn Semester or beginning of Spring Semester (Weeks 15–16 or Weeks 22–23) |

| **3** | Final Thesis (70%), Logbook and Miscellaneous | Deadline | Submit the final thesis (PDF) and project logbook electronically to Moodle. Also, submit your source codes, GUI and other relevant material on Moodle. | 23:59 on 31 March 2027 |
| **4.1** | Thesis Defence (10%) (included in thesis mark) | Deadline | Students should prepare a presentation and demonstrate the functionality of the project. | 23:59 on 30 April 2027 / Anytime between Week 36–Week 37 (students must book this with their moderator) |
| **4.2** | Poster Roadshow Presentation (10%) | Deadline | Presentation session for all Individual Projects. | 4 to 6 May 2027 (students must be available in person) |
| **4.3** | Return of Project Items | Deadline | Return the hardware used for the project to the FYP Lab. We observe this to facilitate the reuse of project items and promote sustainable project management. | Anytime between Week 37–Week 38 |
