## Abstract Summary & Core Mechanics
Presents a methodology for distilling the broad contextual reasoning capabilities of large Vision-Language Models (VLMs) into compact spatial attention maps. These distilled representations feed into a local navigation policy for human-aware social robot navigation, eliminating runtime queries to high-latency VLMs.

---

## Project Relevance & Usage
- **State-of-the-Art Precedent:** Demonstrates that offline VLM-to-navigation distillation is an active, recognized academic paradigm.
- **Clear Research Gap:** ViLAM targets social navigation etiquette and learned neural attention policies in academic hallways, whereas our project targets **brownfield industrial hazards**, distilling into **explicit geometric Nav2 Costmap Filters** on legacy 2D LiDAR AMRs.
