# L12: Probabilistic Robotics

- **Key:** L12
- **Authors:** Sebastian Thrun, Wolfram Burgard, and Dieter Fox
- **Venue / Year:** MIT Press, Cambridge, MA, 2005
- **Status:** T (Canonical textbook reference)

---

## Technical Foundations
The canonical foundation text for probabilistic state estimation and perception in robotics. Specifically details:
1. **Linear and Extended Kalman Filters (EKF):** Recursive state estimation for non-linear process and measurement models.
2. **Kinematic Motion Models:** Constant Velocity (CV) and Constant Turn Rate and Velocity (CTRV) formulations for tracking mobile objects under measurement uncertainty.

---

## Project Relevance & Usage
- **State Estimation Basis (Module 2):** Provides the mathematical formulation for the recursive EKF and motion models tracking pedestrian and obstacle states $(x, y, v_x, v_y, \psi)$ across temporal measurement dropouts.
