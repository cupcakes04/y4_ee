# L06: Florence-2: Advancing a Unified Representation for a Variety of Vision Tasks

- **Key:** L6
- **Authors:** Bin Xiao et al.
- **Venue / Year:** *IEEE/CVF Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition (CVPR)*, 2024

- **Status:** V (arXiv abstract opened and verified)

---

## Abstract Summary & Core Mechanics
Introduces Florence-2, a unified vision foundation model capable of handling diverse spatial and semantic vision tasks (image captioning, object detection, open-vocabulary referring expression grounding, and detailed region segmentation) prompted purely through natural language strings.

---

## Project Relevance & Usage
- **Offline Teacher Model:** Acts as a primary zero-shot teacher in Module 1, taking text prompts (e.g., `"forklift transit lane"`, `"fluid spill"`) and producing high-quality pseudo-ground-truth bounding boxes and masks on unlabelled facility walkthrough videos.
