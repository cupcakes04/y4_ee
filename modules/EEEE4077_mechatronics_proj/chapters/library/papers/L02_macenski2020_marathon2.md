# L02: The Marathon 2: A Navigation System

- **Key:** L2

- **Venue / Year:** *IEEE/RSJ International Conference on Intelligent Robots and Systems (IROS)*, 2020
- **arXiv:** [arXiv:2003.00368](https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.00368)
- **Status:** V (arXiv abstract opened and verified)

---

## Abstract Summary & Core Mechanics
Introduces the next-generation ROS 2 Navigation stack (Nav2). It documents the architectural shift from ROS 1's monolithic `move_base` to a modular, behavior-tree-driven, asynchronous navigation ecosystem designed for high reliability in commercial warehouse and industrial logistics deployments.

---

## Project Relevance & Usage
- **Architecture Baseline:** Grounds the choice of ROS 2 Nav2 as the underlying navigation stack for industrial AMRs.
- **Integration Context:** Validates our behavior-tree recovery integration and costmap layer interfaces.
