# L07: Grounded SAM: Assembling Open-World Models for Diverse Visual Tasks

- **Key:** L7
- **Authors:** Tianhe Ren et al.
- **Venue / Year:** arXiv preprint, 2024
- **arXiv:** [arXiv:2401.14159](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.14159)
- **Status:** V (arXiv abstract opened and verified)

---

## Abstract Summary & Core Mechanics
Assembles Grounding DINO (open-vocabulary text-to-box detection) and the Segment Anything Model (SAM, high-precision promptable mask generation) into an open-world perception framework capable of segmenting complex arbitrary objects from arbitrary text prompts.

---

## Project Relevance & Usage
- **Pseudo-Labelling Engine:** Combined with Florence-2 in our offline dataset pipeline to auto-generate precise hazard and lane boundary polygons from raw walkthrough footage.
