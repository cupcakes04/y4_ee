## Task card: step 2, your robot in Gazebo

**Goal.** Your own robot (diff-drive base, 2D LiDAR, camera) spawned in Gazebo, driven with teleop, with scan and image showing correctly in RViz. By hand: it's your first robot (AGENTS.md §4).

**Done when**
1. `check_urdf` passes on your xacro output.
2. Teleop drives it, and Gazebo and RViz (fixed frame `odom`) move the same way.
3. Scan points lie on the walls in RViz and **stay on them while the robot turns**.
4. `ros2 run tf2_tools view_frames` shows one tree: `odom → … →` every link and sensor frame.
5. The camera image shows in RViz.
6. Rates noted: `/scan`, the image topic, `/odom` (`ros2 topic hz`), and RTF while driving.

**Read** (official examples are templates to adapt, not to admire)
- Gazebo Harmonic docs: *Use ROS 2 to interact with Gazebo* and *Spawn a Gazebo model from ROS 2*.
- `ros_gz_sim_demos` (in the `ros_gz` repo, `jazzy` branch; maybe already installed: `ros2 pkg prefix ros_gz_sim_demos`): the diff-drive, gpu_lidar and camera demos.
- Your ROS2_notes 07 (URDF, xacro, robot_state_publisher).

**You decide** (one line each in Notes, with the reason)
- The frame layout: root link, where `base_link` sits, sensor heights. Q3 says a 0.3 m scan plane hits actors [M:q3_lidar_actor].
- Camera resolution, FoV, rate (the laptop iGPU renders it: Q11).
- Lidar noise yes/no (left open at step 1).

**Traps** (they cost hours and teach little)
- Gazebo merges links on fixed joints into their parent. Set `<gz_frame_id>` on each sensor, or its data gets stamped with the wrong frame.
- The world needs the Sensors system, or sensors publish nothing (step 1). The Q3 world's plugin block works.
- The diff-drive system's odom transform and the wheel joint states live on Gazebo topics. Bridge them to `/tf` and `/joint_states`, or odom and the wheels go missing in RViz.
- Stamp images with an optical frame (z forward, x right, y down). M4's projection maths assumes it.
- Give both wheel joints the same axis direction: the diff-drive system expects positive = forward for both.
- Every moving link needs mass and inertia in Gazebo.

**Who types what.** package.xml, CMake install lines, launch skeleton: ask the agent or copy from the demos. Frame tree, sensors, bridge topics, launch wiring: you.
