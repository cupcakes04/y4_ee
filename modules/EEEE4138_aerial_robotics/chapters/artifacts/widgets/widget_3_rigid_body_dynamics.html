2. Click the "+Pitch Impulse (+0.5 N·m)" button below.

4. The Math: Pitching rotating blades ($q > 0$) causes their spin angular momentum $H_z$ to precess, generating an orthogonal gyroscopic reaction moment: $\tau_{\text{gyro, roll}} = -q J_r \Omega_{\text{net}}$.
