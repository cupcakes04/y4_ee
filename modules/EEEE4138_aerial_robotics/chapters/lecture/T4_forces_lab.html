The one idea: each rotor does two separate things

Thrust at its corner → roll, pitch

r × F = [x y 0] × [0 0 −T] = [−yT, xT, 0]

Depends on where the rotor sits. A rotor lifts its own corner: front rotor → nose up (+My), left rotor → left side up (+Mx). Tut 3 Q2, sl.20/24.

Drag torque → yaw only

M_z,i = σᵢ k_MT Tᵢ, k_MT = k_M / k_T [m]

Depends on which way it spins. The air drags the blades, and the frame twists opposite to the spin: a CCW rotor (σ = +1) → frame turns CW from above → +Mz, nose right. sl.12–15.

2 · Backward: wanted loads → thrusts (allocation, used by the controller)

      
The controller (T5) decides "I need Fz and these three moments". The motors only take speeds. Allocation inverts A: t = A⁻¹[Fz Mx My Mz]ᵀ, then Ωᵢ = √(Tᵢ/k_T), then check 0 ≤ Tᵢ ≤ T_max for every rotor (sl.21, 25).

      
controller (T5)→ Fz Mx My Mz →A⁻¹ (allocation)→ Tᵢ →Ωᵢ = √(Tᵢ/k_T), clip→ Ωᵢ →RotorLoads: A t (panel 1)→ Fz Mx My Mz →StateDerivative (T3)
