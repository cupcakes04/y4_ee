---

You are auditing a final-year engineering project (thesis + ROS 2 / Nav2 / Gazebo code), built by a student working with another agent. Read as widely as you can: everything in `modules/EEEE4077_mechatronics_proj/`, including the old proposal phase in `archive/` and the university requirements in `raw/` (start with `.agents/fyp/AGENTS.md` and `chapters/notes/brief.md` for orientation). Then tell us what we are missing: anything you find. Judge freely; nothing is off limits, including decisions already made.

Change nothing in the repo except one file: write your report to `modules/EEEE4077_mechatronics_proj/chapters/audits/<YYYY-MM-DD>.md`. For each finding, point to where you saw it (file and line, or the command you ran), so it can be checked quickly.
